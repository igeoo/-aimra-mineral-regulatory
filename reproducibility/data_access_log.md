# Data Access Log

This log distinguishes public, structured-estimate, and restricted data categories.

## Public or publicly traceable sources

- DMRE Annual Report 2021/22
- EITI South Africa Reconciliation Report 2022
- NEITI Solid Minerals Annual Report 2022
- AfDB Revenue Governance Index 2022
- World Bank Worldwide Governance Indicators
- Alberta/Canadian public regulatory records
- Utah public regulatory records

## Restricted or non-redistributed sources for Stage 2

- NUPRC enforcement and concession-level case logs
- Ondo State environmental inspection records
- Regulator-level licensing and response-time records
- Human review panel and audit-trail records generated during pilot deployment

## Access strategy

Restricted records should be accessed through formal institutional data-sharing agreements, ethics clearance, and clearly defined confidentiality protocols. Such records should not be redistributed publicly unless permission is explicitly granted by the data owner.
